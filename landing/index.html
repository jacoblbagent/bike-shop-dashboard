✦ What It Does

Everything a bike shop needs, nothing it doesn't

ChainLink is built from the ground up for the way bike shops actually work. No bloated ERP — just the tools you reach for every day.

Dashboard & Analytics

See your revenue, orders, and customer metrics at a glance. Interactive charts with custom date ranges help you spot trends without digging through reports.

Inventory (Bikes & Parts)

Track mountain, road, hybrid, and electric bikes plus every part category — brakes, drivetrain, wheels, suspension, and more. Low-stock alerts keep you from running out.

Customer CRM

Know your riders. Track spend, order history, and loyalty tier (Bronze through Platinum). Filter by tier, search, and see who hasn't visited lately.

Sales & Orders

Ring up sales with line-item details. Seven order statuses, six payment methods — search, sort, and filter your full order history.

Purchase Orders

Manage supplier orders from Draft to Received. Partial receiving means you can mark items as they arrive. Track lead times and expected dates.

Reports

Top products, inventory value by category, customer metrics, and sales summaries — generated for whatever period you need.

✦ Built For

Works great with the brands you carry

ChainLink is brand-agnostic — track inventory for Specialized, Yeti, Giant, Norco, Trek, Cannondale, or anything else your shop stocks.
